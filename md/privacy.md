# Privacy & cookies

How Priority Ads Agency handles your information on this website. Last updated: 26 November 2026.

## What we collect

This website has no accounts or logins and keeps no database of visitors. Our contact form does not send anything to our server. It opens WhatsApp with your message ready, and you decide whether to send it. When you message, call or email us, we receive the details you choose to share (such as your name, phone number, business and message) and use them only to reply to you and discuss our services.

## Cookies we use

We only set non-essential cookies if you press **Accept** on the cookie banner. If you choose **Only necessary** , no tracking tools are loaded.

| Cookie | Provider | Purpose | Kept for | Type |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| pa\_consent | Priority Ads Agency | Remembers your cookie choice | 180 days | Necessary |
| \_ga, \_ga\_\* | Google Analytics | Counts visits and shows which pages are useful (no names or phone numbers) | Up to 2 years | Analytics: only after Accept |
| \_fbp, \_fbc | Meta (Facebook/Instagram) | Measures our ads and shows you relevant ads on Facebook and Instagram | Up to 90 days | Marketing: only after Accept |

Google and Meta process this data under their own policies: [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [Meta Privacy Policy](https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policy/).

## Changing your choice

You can change or withdraw your consent at any time with the "Cookie settings" link at the bottom of every page . You can also delete cookies in your browser settings.

## Links to other sites

Our pages link to WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, X, Google Maps and our courses site. Those services have their own privacy policies.
