## Questions about WhatsApp Marketing

Do I need the WhatsApp Business app?

The free WhatsApp Business app works for most local businesses. We set it up with your catalogue, labels and quick replies.

Can you send messages to people who haven't contacted me?

We only message customers who have opted in, which keeps your number safe and your messages welcome.

How fast do I need to reply?

Within minutes is best. Auto-replies cover the gap, and we suggest a simple routine so no chat goes cold.

Which businesses suit WhatsApp marketing?

Restaurants, clinics, salons, real estate, coaching and any business where customers like to ask before they buy.
