Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about booking and our styling process.

How early do I need to book my event date? ▼

We recommend booking 2 to 4 weeks in advance, especially for weekend party dates (Fridays, Saturdays, Sundays) as our schedule fills quickly. However, we also accommodate last-minute bookings subject to availability.

How long does the setup take on event day? ▼

Our typical setup time ranges from 1.5 to 3 hours depending on your chosen package (Basic packages take ~1.5 hours, while grand Luxury & Royal setups take 2.5 to 3 hours). We coordinate directly with your venue or home timing.

Do you provide teardown and clean-up after the party? ▼

Yes, absolutely! All our packages include complete teardown. We will collect rented arch backdrops, pedestals, neon signage, and framing at the conclusion of your event or the following morning, leaving the space pristine.

Can I customize the balloon colors and backdrop sign text? ▼

100% yes! We specialize in bespoke styling. You can choose any color palette, and we custom produce the decal text or neon lighting with the celebrant’s name, age, or special wording.

What happens if I need to change my event date? ▼

We offer hassle-free date rescheduling up to 7 days before your celebration. Your deposit transfers directly to your new available date without any penalty fees.
