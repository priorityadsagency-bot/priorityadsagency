Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our health standards, booking process, and boarding facilities.

What pet care services do you provide? expand_more
We provide full-spectrum pet wellness including holistic pet grooming & spa sessions, preventive veterinary care, boutique luxury boarding suites, personalized dog walking adventures, certified positive-reinforcement behavior training, and supervised daytime socialization daycare.
How can I book an appointment? expand_more
Booking can be completed online via our 24/7 web booking portal or over the phone with our client care team at +1 (800) PAW-CARE. Simply choose your service, pick your preferred caregiver or time slot, and specify your pet's routine requirements.
Are your pet caregivers trained? expand_more
Yes, every single member of our team undergoes an intensive 80-hour animal behavior program, is CPR & First-Aid certified by veterinarian clinicians, passes comprehensive federal background screenings, and carries full bonded insurance.
Do you provide emergency assistance? expand_more
We maintain an on-call veterinarian partner network active 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. For boarding and daycare guests, we have immediate emergency medical transport ready and prompt notification protocols to reach you without delay.
Can I customize my pet's care plan? expand_more
Absolutely. No two pets are identical. We encourage parents to outline exact medication schedules, dietary choices (raw, vegan, allergen-free), low-stimulus environments, and customized walking paces during the digital onboarding consultation.
