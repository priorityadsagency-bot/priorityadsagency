Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to book a table in advance? +

We highly recommend reserving in advance, particularly for weekend evenings and festive celebrations, to guarantee your preferred seating.

Can I order food online for home delivery? +

Yes, you can order directly through our website or via our concierge hotline. We offer pristine insulated delivery within a 10 km radius.

Are there vegetarian options available? +

Absolutely. We feature an extensive gourmet vegetarian and vegan tasting menu crafted alongside our classical dishes.

Do you offer special arrangements for events? +

Yes, our private dining salon accommodates corporate gatherings, intimate anniversaries, and family celebrations with bespoke set menus.

Can I modify or cancel my reservation? +

You can reschedule or cancel at any time up to 2 hours before your scheduled booking via your confirmation link or WhatsApp hotline.
