Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book an appointment? +

You can easily book online via our booking form above, call our reception directly, or send us a WhatsApp message.

Which treatments do you offer? +

We provide teeth whitening, dental implants, aligners & braces, root canals, cosmetic smile designing, and preventive cleanings.

Is the treatment painful? +

No. We use modern, gentle anaesthesia techniques and minimally invasive technology to ensure your visits are virtually painless.

Do you provide care for children? +

Yes, our paediatric dental specialists ensure children feel relaxed, happy, and well cared for.
