Parent Guidance & Clarifications

Frequently Asked Questions

Transparent answers to help you make an informed decision for your child's academic future.

How do I register for a free demo session? expand_more

Simply fill out the inquiry form at the top or bottom of this page. Our academic counselors will verify your grade and subject preferences, after which a 2-day free guest demo pass will be issued for both in-person or live online batches.

What is the average batch size per class? expand_more

We operate with a strict cap of 15 to 20 students per cohort. This ensures that every child receives active attention, their homework is individually scrutinized, and shyer students have the space to ask questions without hesitation.

Are installment options available for fee payments? expand_more

Yes. We offer zero-cost monthly and quarterly installment plans (EMI) with leading educational finance partners. Additionally, meritorious students can claim up to 40% fee remission via our weekly diagnostic scholarship test.

How are parents kept updated regarding attendance and test scores? expand_more

Parents receive daily automated SMS/WhatsApp alerts confirming check-in attendance. Furthermore, detailed graphical test scorecards are published bi-weekly on the student portal, complemented by structured bi-monthly parent-teacher meetings.

What happens if a student misses a lecture? expand_more

All core lectures are recorded in high-definition audio and video. Missed lectures are instantly accessible in the student archive portal. Weekend 1-on-1 backup tutoring slots are also scheduled on Saturday mornings to resolve missed concepts.

Do you provide study material and past test papers? expand_more

Yes. Enrolled students receive comprehensive printed chapter modules, formula handbooks, 10-year past solved exam papers, and daily practice problem (DPP) sets fully updated with the latest 2025–26 syllabus guidelines.
